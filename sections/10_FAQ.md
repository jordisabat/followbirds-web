# Section: FAQ

## Purpose

Answer common objections and clarify product scope.

## Suggested Questions

### Is FollowBirds only for Algarve?

FollowBirds is starting in Algarve, Portugal. The goal is to become excellent locally before expanding to other regions.

### Can guides use it for tours?

Yes. FollowBirds is being designed around guide workflows: tour templates, shared observations, live checklists and automatic reports.

### Can birdwatchers use it without joining a tour?

Yes. Birdwatchers can explore birds, locations and checklists independently.

### Does it work offline?

The app is offline-first. Bird and location content should remain useful even without connection.

### Are reports automatic?

The planned report system generates web/PDF reports from tour observations, species lists, photos, notes and route information.

### Is the guide program available now?

The guide program is intended for early professional guides in Algarve. Final signup flow may be added later.

### Is the app free for birdwatchers?

Birdwatchers are secondary users. Final pricing should remain flexible until the guide business model is implemented.

## Implementation

Use either:

- static FAQ list
- accessible accordion

If using accordion, avoid heavy JavaScript.

## Avoid

- Overpromising exact pricing
- Claiming worldwide coverage
