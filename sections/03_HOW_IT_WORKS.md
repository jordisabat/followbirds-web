# Section: How It Works

## Purpose

Explain the core workflow simply.

## Structure

Use 3 cards.

### 1. Create Tour

Guides prepare reusable tour templates and invite participants.

### 2. Share Observations

During the tour, guides record species, notes, photos, time and location.

### 3. Generate Reports

After the tour, FollowBirds creates a shareable web/PDF report automatically.
