# Section: For Birdwatchers

## Purpose

Explain the value for participants and normal users.

Birdwatchers are important, but secondary to guides in business positioning.

## Suggested Headline

> Your birding memories, saved forever.

## Suggested Copy

Join guided tours, follow the shared species list, mark your own sightings and keep every report in your personal birding history.

## Feature Cards

- Join by invitation link or QR
- Follow live species list
- Mark seen / photographed / lifer / favorite
- Save personal checklists
- Keep reports and history

## CTA

- Download App

## Visual

Use:

- Participant phone mockup
- Checklist UI
- Birdwatcher viewing species list

## Avoid

- Making this section more important than guides
- Overpromising features not planned
