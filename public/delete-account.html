Request Account Deletion — followbirds

followbirds uses Google Sign-In for authentication. To request deletion of your account and all associated data:

  1. Send an email to info@followbirds.com
  2. Include the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. In the body, provide the email address associated with your followbirds account (the Google account you used to sign in)

We will process your request within 7 days and confirm once completed.

What data is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and linked Google identity
  • All your checklists and checklist data
  • Any feedback or missing bird reports you submitted

What data may be retained

  • Anonymous analytics events (not linked to your identity)
  • Aggregated, anonymized usage statistics

For questions, contact info@followbirds.com.
